DESIGNED FOR ACCOUNTABILITY

Trust is a control, not a badge.

This demo is not certified production infrastructure. Every boundary below is explicit.

Data & sovereignty

Invented companies, orders, prices and capacity. Public CC0 city reference facts are attributed below.

West Europe demonstration only. All-data-and-inference-in-Türkiye production operation requires an approved Türkiye-hosted platform and legal/regulatory assessment.

Execution boundaries

Change windows, route-conflict holds, one-attempt recovery playbooks, idempotent orders and atomic persisted state transitions.

Optional live Azure OpenAI advice has no execution authority. Rules simulation is always clearly labelled.

Hash-linked audit records detect simple edits; they are not immutable regulatory evidence or a digital signature.
