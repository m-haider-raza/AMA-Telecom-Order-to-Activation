Solution scope and delivery requirements

This film presents a customer solution proposal. Azure Local at an approved Turkish operator site hosts customer workloads and inference, with local keys, logs and backups. Outbound metadata flows are reviewed and controlled.

TM Forum-inspired product, service and resource contracts keep the order journey linked. The trusted boundary controls state changes and Azure Communication Services email. Customers sign in to see their own orders; partner access is organisation-scoped. Bounded recovery hands other faults to an accountable service desk.

Each agent has a distinct Entra identity and only the projects, tools and operations it needs. Receiving APIs enforce caller identity, role and current policy. Service managers use personal Entra sign-in; agents and partners cannot grant approvals.

Microsoft Agent 365 is part of the proposed agent-governance solution, providing inventory, ownership visibility and oversight. Named business sponsors approve purpose, risk and budget; technical owners control releases and incidents. Audit trails, access reviews, alerts and identity revocation complement server-enforced permissions.

Microsoft product icons identify their services. Operator branding supplies industry context; it implies no endorsement, relationship or actual carrier integration.
