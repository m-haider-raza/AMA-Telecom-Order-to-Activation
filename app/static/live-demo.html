    What this demo proves, and what it does not

    It demonstrates linked orders, policy holds, one allowlisted timeout retry, allocate-once guards, audited human decisions and persisted Turkish/English portal messages. Unknown-fault triage and real carrier/channel integrations remain production work.

    TMF622/TMF641-inspired subsets are not certified full TM Forum contracts. MCP grants no approval authority. This app does not deploy Azure Local or establish Turkish data residency. Production requires approved infrastructure and data/inference/backup controls inside Türkiye.
