Solution scope and delivery requirements

This customer walkthrough describes the solution design and illustrative workflow. The running Azure reference implementation uses synthetic data and simulated carrier adapters. Türkiye residency requires supported Azure Local infrastructure at an approved Turkish site, local application services, inference, keys, logs and backups, and approved egress controls. Connected Arc platform metadata requires review; disconnected operation requires eligibility and capacity.

Business objectives describe the intended value, rather than a quantified customer outcome. TMF622/TMF641-inspired interfaces require customer-specific contracts and conformance acceptance. The customer portal is the demonstrated message channel; external delivery needs integrated channel services. Unknown-fault escalation requires a service-desk integration and accountable operational owner.

Microsoft product icons identify their services. Operator branding supplies industry context; it implies no endorsement, relationship or actual carrier integration.
